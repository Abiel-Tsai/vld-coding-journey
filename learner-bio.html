Hello, I’m a coding learner.

I am a high-school student exploring coding through independent online learning. I enjoy understanding how things work, solving problems, and improving an idea through experimentation.

My goal this year is to become more confident working through difficulty. Instead of expecting code to work immediately, I want to read errors carefully, test one change at a time, and explain the reasoning behind my solutions.
