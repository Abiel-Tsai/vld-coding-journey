Learner bio

A brief, privacy-safe introduction to who I am as a learner.

learn();

I’m a student learning how to build things with code.

I enjoy understanding how things work, solving problems, and improving an idea through experimentation.

My goal this year is to become more confident working through difficulty. Instead of expecting code to work immediately, I want to read errors carefully, test one change at a time, and explain the reasoning behind my solutions.

How I learn

  • Strength

    Curiosity

    I ask why something works rather than stopping when it works.

  • Developing

    Persistence

    I am practicing staying with difficult problems instead of switching to easier work.

  • Best condition

    Making projects

    I learn best when I can apply a concept in something small and useful.
